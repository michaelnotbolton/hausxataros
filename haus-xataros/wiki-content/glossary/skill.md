# Skill

A Skill is learned competence — the focused knowledge and practice that supplements raw [[stat|Stat]] potential. Where the Stat is what your body, mind, or presence *can* do in the broadest sense, a Skill is what you have trained it to do *specifically*. Each rank of a relevant Skill adds +2 dice to the [[dice-hand|Dice Hand]] for actions that fall within its domain. A character with Physical 3 and Melee 2 rolls 3 base dice plus 4 Skill dice — 7 total before equipment. Skills are the difference between someone who is strong and someone who knows how to fight.

---

## Skill Dice

> **Skill contribution = Skill Rank × 2 dice**

Each Skill rank adds 2 dice to the Dice Hand when performing an action the Skill covers. Multiple Skills can apply to a single action, up to a cap of **4 Skills per action**. A character with Melee 2 and Athletics 1 performing a leaping sword strike could apply both — 4 dice from Melee plus 2 from Athletics, for 6 Skill dice total.

The Dice Hand is still subject to the [[entropic-cap|Entropic Cap]]. High Skill investment in a low-Pool domain gets capped — the Skills provide the dice, but the Pool sets the ceiling.

---

## Automatic Skills

At character creation, each [[stat|Stat]] rank grants a number of Automatic Skills in that Stat's domain. These represent the baseline competencies that come naturally with higher capability — a character with Mental 4 knows more things than a character with Mental 1, regardless of what either has deliberately trained.

Additional Skills can be purchased with [[cp|CP]] at creation or [[xp|XP]] during play (2 XP per Skill rank).

---

## Skills in Combat and Beyond

Skills contribute dice to:

- **Combat actions** — Melee adds to sword strikes, Firearms adds to gunshots, Rhetoric adds to verbal attacks.
- **[[scene-action|Scene Actions]]** — Athletics adds to chases, Investigation adds to searches, Medicine adds to field treatment.
- **Skill checks** — Any non-combat roll where the Judge determines a Skill applies.

The same Skill contributes the same +2 dice per rank regardless of context. There is no separate "combat skill" and "exploration skill" — if Melee applies, Melee contributes.

---

## You Died Variant

> **You Died sub-game:** In You Died, Skills add bonus chips to the domain-specific chip pool rather than dice to the Dice Hand. The per-rank bonus and 4-Skill cap per action apply identically.
