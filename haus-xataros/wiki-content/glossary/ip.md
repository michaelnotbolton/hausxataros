# IP (Item Points)

IP is the equipment currency — the budget reserved exclusively for purchasing [[item|Items]] at character creation. Starting budget: **15 IP**. IP is deliberately separated from [[cp|CP]] to prevent a character from dumping all creation resources into combat [[power|Powers]] while ignoring equipment entirely (or vice versa). The separation enforces breadth: every character has a gear budget and a capability budget, and neither can cannibalize the other.

---

## What IP Buys

IP purchases Items during character creation. Each Item has a cost determined by its Quality rating, tags, and complications. After creation, Items are acquired through gameplay, crafting, or [[xp|XP]] expenditure — IP is a creation-only currency.

---

## You Died Variant

> **You Died sub-game:** In You Died, character construction uses the same IP budget and separation from CP. Chip-based Items follow the same Quality and cost structure.
