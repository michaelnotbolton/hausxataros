# Pool

You have three lives, and they are all running at once. Your Physical Pool is the integrity of your body. Your Mental Pool is the coherence of your mind. Your Social Pool is the substance of your identity — your composure, your reputation, the part of you that other people recognize. In Jotunnberg, a knife to the ribs and a perfectly delivered insult draw from different reserves, but both reserves run out the same way: at zero, you are done.

---

## The Three Pools

Every character has three Pools, each tied to a core [[stat|Stat]]:

| Pool | Governs | When It Hits Zero |
|:-----|:--------|:------------------|
| **Physical** | Body, endurance, health | Unconscious, broken, bleeding out |
| **Mental** | Focus, willpower, cognitive coherence | Catatonic, dissociated, overwhelmed |
| **Social** | Composure, reputation, presence | Identity dissolved, publicly unmade |

All three are mechanically equivalent. Social damage is not "soft" damage. It is not a debuff. When someone reduces your Social Pool to zero through rhetoric, intimidation, or a precisely worded insult, you are removed from the scene just as thoroughly as if they had hit you with a bus. The MRE does not have "real" combat and "social" combat. It has combat.

---

## Pool Formula

> **Pool = (Stat Rank × 6) + 6**

| Stat Rank | Pool Value |
|:---------:|:---------:|
| 1 | 12 |
| 2 | 18 |
| 3 | 24 |
| 4 | 30 |
| 5 | 36 |

The +6 baseline means even a Stat 1 character has a meaningful (if fragile) Pool. No Pool starts at zero. But a Pool of 12 is thin enough that a single well-placed attack can halve it — and once the [[entropic-cap|Entropic Cap]] kicks in, that halved Pool means a halved [[dice-hand|Dice Hand]], which means everything gets harder from there.

---

## How Pools Change

**Damage** reduces a Pool. Attacks, environmental hazards, and [[strain|Strain]] (self-damage from using Powers) all subtract from a specific Pool. The attacker chooses which Pool to target based on the type of action — a punch targets Physical, a psychic assault targets Mental, a public humiliation targets Social.

**Healing** restores a Pool. Healing effects add back to the Pool, but cannot exceed the Pool's maximum value. The [[strain|Strain]] Thermodynamic Law applies: healing cannot restore Strain damage dealt in the same Beat. Power has a cost that cannot be circumvented within the same time window.

**The Entropic Cap** means that Pool damage is capability damage. Your [[dice-hand|Dice Hand]] cannot exceed your current Pool value. As you take damage, your options shrink — fewer dice for [[momentum|Momentum]], fewer for Defense, fewer for Action. The first wound creates pressure toward the second.

---

## Gate State

When any single Pool reaches 0, the character enters [[gate-state|Gate State]] — scene removal. They are functionally unable to continue participating in the current conflict. Gate State also makes the character vulnerable to [[exotic-tag|Exotic Tags]] (Domination, Erasure, Execution) — the MRE's most devastating effects, locked behind the requirement that you break someone before you can finish them.

---

## Strategic Notes

**Pool targeting is the game within the game.** A character with Physical 5 (Pool 36) and Social 1 (Pool 12) is a fortress in one domain and glass in another. If you know which Pool is small, you know where to push. Information-gathering powers are not optional — they are fight-winners.

**Strain routing matters.** Every Power designates a Strain Pool. A character who routes all their Strain to their Social Pool is protecting their Physical combat capability at the cost of their social resilience. If an enemy switches to Social attacks, that Strain routing becomes a liability.

## You Died Variant

> **You Died sub-game:** In You Died, Pools are tracked as chip totals rather than numeric values. The three-domain structure and Gate State trigger at zero remain identical. Chip costs replace dice costs throughout.
