# Restriction Bonus

The Restriction Bonus is what you get for telling the universe exactly who you are — and accepting the vulnerability that comes with it. When a [[power|Power]] carries [[restriction|Restrictions]] (conditions that limit how, when, or where it can be used), those Restrictions grant two benefits: additional [[effect-tag|Effect Ranks]] (making the Power stronger) and reduced [[strain|Strain]] dice (making it cheaper to use). The Restriction Bonus is the mechanical heart of [[creative-reciprocity|Creative Reciprocity]]: commitment to identity is both strength and risk, and the engine rewards both equally.

---

## The Math

Restrictions grant bonuses on a diminishing scale:

| Restriction Position | Effect Rank Bonus | Strain Reduction |
|:--------------------:|:-----------------:|:----------------:|
| 1st | +1 | −1d6 |
| 2nd | +1 | −1d6 |
| 3rd | +1 | −1d6 |
| 4th | +1 | −1d6 |
| 5th–6th | +1 per 2 | — |
| 7th–8th | +1 per 2 | — |
| 9th+ | +1 per 4 | — |

The first 4 Restrictions are the sweet spot: each grants a full +1 Effect Rank and −1 Strain Die. A 1-[[intensity|Intensity]] Power with 4 Restrictions has 5 Effect Ranks and costs 1d6 Strain (the minimum floor). A 5-Intensity Power with 0 Restrictions also has 5 Effect Ranks but costs 5d6 Strain. Same output. Five times the self-damage. This is the [[creative-reciprocity|1 CP Nuke]] in action.

Beyond 4, diminishing returns kick in. Restrictions 5–8 grant 1 Effect Rank per 2 Restrictions applied. Restrictions 9+ grant 1 per 4. Strain reduction caps after the first 4. This curve discourages stacking hyper-specific, rarely-applicable Restrictions while still allowing deep commitment for characters whose identity demands it.

---

## The Tradeoff

Every Restriction is a condition an informed opponent can exploit. A Power restricted to "only usable while standing" fails the moment you're knocked prone. A Power restricted to "requires verbal component" fails the moment you're silenced. The Restriction Bonus rewards vulnerability — but the vulnerability is real. An enemy who learns your Restrictions can systematically deny your strongest tools.

This is why information matters in the MRE. Knowing an opponent's Restrictions is knowing how to shut them down. The [[specificity-modifier|Specificity Modifier]] and the Restriction Bonus together create a system where the most powerful characters are also the most *readable* characters — and reading them correctly is the path to beating them.

---

## You Died Variant

> **You Died sub-game:** In You Died, the Restriction Bonus operates identically — Restrictions grant bonus chip-draw ranks and reduce chip-cost per use. The diminishing returns scale is unchanged.
