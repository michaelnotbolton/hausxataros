# Allocation Phase

This is where the hand gets played. Before a single die is rolled, before anyone knows what anyone else intends, every combatant takes their [[dice-hand|Dice Hand]] and divides it — secretly, simultaneously, irrevocably — into the three commitments that will define their Beat. Speed, survival, or force. You cannot have all three. You will not know what your opponent chose until it is too late to change your mind.

---

## The Phase

The Allocation Phase is Phase 2 of the [[poker-hand-model|Poker-Hand Model]], following the [[planning-phase|Planning Phase]] and preceding the [[reveal-phase|Reveal Phase]].

Each player takes their complete [[dice-hand|Dice Hand]] for the Beat and divides it into three allocations:

| Allocation | Purpose |
|:-----------|:--------|
| **[[momentum|Momentum]]** | Purchases [[tempo|Tempo]] (priority manipulation) and [[flashstep|Flashstep]] (additional actions). The only sub-pool that buys speed. |
| **Defense** | Sets the [[threshold|Threshold]] attackers must overcome to hit you. More Defense dice = harder to hit. |
| **Action** | The dice you actually roll when using [[power|Powers]] or performing [[scene-action|Scene Actions]]. |

All three allocations must total exactly the Dice Hand. Each may be 0.

---

## How It Works

**Method:** Players write their allocations on paper, use a screen, or cup dice behind their hand. The Judge allocates for all NPCs simultaneously. Nobody sees anyone else's allocation until the [[reveal-phase|Reveal Phase]].

**[[employment|Employment]] declaration:** If using equipped [[item|Items]] this Beat, subtract 1 die per Item employed from the Dice Hand *before* allocation begins. A character employing a sword (−1) and a shield (−1) loses 2 dice from their total hand before dividing what remains.

**[[flashstep|Flashstep]] commitment:** If purchasing Flashstep tiers, those Momentum dice are committed during Allocation. The specific tier costs are provisional (see [[flashstep|Flashstep]]), but the commitment is binding — once allocated, the Momentum spent on Flashstep cannot be redirected.

**[[tempo|Tempo]] commitment:** If purchasing Tempo, those Momentum dice are also committed during Allocation at the rate of 3 Momentum dice per 1 Tempo. Tempo and Flashstep draw from the same Momentum sub-pool.

---

## The Core Tension

The Allocation Phase is the engine of the MRE's combat drama. It creates three interlocking dilemmas:

**Speed vs. Power.** Every die in Momentum is a die not in Action. A character who loads Momentum for Flashstep tier 2 and high Tempo has very few dice left for Action — their attacks land early but hit softly.

**Speed vs. Survival.** Every die in Momentum is a die not in Defense. A character buying heavy speed has a low [[threshold|Threshold]] — easy to hit, easy to damage.

**Power vs. Survival.** Every die in Action is a die not in Defense. A character who stacks Action for a devastating hit is wide open if the opponent survives.

There are no free resources. The hand is the budget. The budget is the drama.

---

## The Blind Bet

Allocations are made blind. You do not know whether your opponent stacked Defense (making your big Action commitment irrelevant) or loaded Momentum (meaning they'll act before you and you should have invested in survival). This is the poker hand: you commit based on your read of the opponent, and you find out who read better at the [[reveal-phase|Reveal]].

---

## You Died Variant

> **You Died sub-game:** In You Died, the Allocation Phase uses chip pools instead of dice. Players divide their domain-specific chip total into the same three buckets (speed, defense, action). The blind-commitment structure is identical — allocations are hidden until simultaneous reveal.
