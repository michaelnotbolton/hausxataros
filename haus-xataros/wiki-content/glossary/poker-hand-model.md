# Poker-Hand Model

The Poker-Hand Model is the MRE's simultaneous combat resolution system — the five-phase cycle that turns every Beat into a hand of cards where nobody knows what anyone else is holding until it's too late to change their mind. Named for the secret allocation mechanic at its heart, the model transforms combat from "I hit, you hit" into a structured game of commitment, prediction, and consequence. You read your opponent. You play your hand. You find out who read better. Then you live with it.

---

## The Five Phases

Each [[beat|Beat]] executes the five phases in order:

| Phase | Name | What Happens |
|:-----:|:-----|:-------------|
| 1 | **[[planning-phase|Planning Phase]]** | Open strategy discussion. Toggle Persistent State Powers. Spend [[spontaneous-evolution|Spontaneous Evolution]] XP. Declare Flashstep intentions. |
| 2 | **[[allocation-phase|Allocation Phase]]** | Secretly divide your [[dice-hand|Dice Hand]] into [[momentum|Momentum]], Defense, and Action. Commit [[tempo|Tempo]] and [[flashstep|Flashstep]] purchases. |
| 3 | **[[reveal-phase|Reveal Phase]]** | All allocations revealed simultaneously. No changes permitted. |
| 4 | **[[resolution-phase|Resolution Phase]]** | Actions resolve by [[time-bands|Time Band]], then by Momentum order within each band. Dice are rolled. [[damage|Damage]] is dealt. |
| 5 | **[[cleanup-phase|Cleanup Phase]]** | [[strain|Strain]] is rolled. Cooldowns tick. [[gate-state|Gate State]] is checked. [[spontaneous-evolution|Spontaneous Evolution]] is processed. Reset. |

After Phase 5, the cycle returns to Phase 1 for the next Beat. Combat continues until one side is eliminated, surrenders, or the scene's dramatic question is resolved.

---

## Why "Poker-Hand"

The name comes from Phase 2 — the Allocation Phase. Every combatant takes the same finite resource (their Dice Hand) and divides it into three hidden commitments (speed, survival, force) without knowing what anyone else chose. The Reveal Phase is the showdown. The Resolution Phase is the consequences.

This is not turn-based combat. It is not initiative-order combat. It is simultaneous decision-making with hidden information, resolved through a structured sequence. The drama lives in the gap between what you *thought* your opponent would do and what they *actually* did.

---

## The Structural Promise

The Poker-Hand Model guarantees three things:

**Every Beat is a decision.** There is no "I attack" default. Every Beat requires a fresh allocation — a fresh read of the situation, a fresh commitment of resources. Autopilot is structurally impossible because the allocation is the decision, and the allocation changes every Beat.

**Information is always imperfect.** You never know your opponent's allocation until the Reveal. You can observe their past patterns, note their remaining Pool, estimate their likely strategy — but you are always guessing. The model rewards pattern recognition and punishes rigidity.

**Consequences compound.** Through the [[entropic-cap|Entropic Cap]], every point of damage shrinks the Dice Hand, which shrinks the allocation options, which makes the next Beat's decisions harder. The model does not reset between Beats. It degrades.

## You Died Variant

> **You Died sub-game:** In You Died, the Poker-Hand Model operates identically using chip pools instead of dice. The five-phase structure, simultaneous allocation, and hidden-information reveal are preserved. Chip costs replace die counts throughout.
