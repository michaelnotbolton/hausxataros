# Beat

A Beat is the basic unit of game time in the MRE — one complete cycle of the [[poker-hand-model|Poker-Hand Model]], from [[planning-phase|Planning]] through [[cleanup-phase|Cleanup]]. It is not a fixed number of seconds. It is not a round in the traditional sense. A Beat is the span in which every combatant commits once, reveals once, and lives with the consequences once. Multiple Beats compose a [[scene|Scene]]. The fight lasts as many Beats as it takes for someone to break — or for the dramatic question to resolve.

---

## The Five Phases of a Beat

| Phase | Name | Function |
|:-----:|:-----|:---------|
| 1 | [[planning-phase|Planning]] | Open discussion, Persistent State toggles, [[spontaneous-evolution|Spontaneous Evolution]] XP spending |
| 2 | [[allocation-phase|Allocation]] | Secret division of the [[dice-hand|Dice Hand]] into [[momentum|Momentum]], Defense, Action |
| 3 | [[reveal-phase|Reveal]] | Simultaneous exposure of all allocations |
| 4 | [[resolution-phase|Resolution]] | Actions resolve by [[time-bands|Time Band]] and Momentum order |
| 5 | [[cleanup-phase|Cleanup]] | [[strain|Strain]] rolled, [[gate-state|Gate State]] checked, Spontaneous Evolution processed |

After Phase 5, the cycle resets to Phase 1 for the next Beat.

---

## Beat as Dramatic Unit

The MRE uses "Beat" instead of "Round" deliberately. A Beat is a unit of dramatic rhythm, not chronological measurement. Two characters exchanging sword blows and two characters exchanging legal arguments both occupy a single Beat — the system does not distinguish between physical and temporal scale. What matters is that every participant made one set of decisions and faced one set of consequences. That is the Beat.

## You Died Variant

> **You Died sub-game:** In You Died, a Beat follows the same five-phase structure using chip pools instead of dice. The dramatic-unit framing is identical.
