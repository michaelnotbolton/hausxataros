# Keyword

A Keyword is the elemental name a [[power|Power]] wears — the label that transforms it from generic force into typed identity. "Kinetic." "Arc." "Psychic." "Void." When a Power carries a Keyword, the [[specificity-modifier|Specificity Modifier]] activates: offensive output doubles, defensive output increases by half, and the Power enters the type matchup system of the [[hexagon-circuit|Hexagon]] and [[triangle-circuit|Triangle Circuits]]. Keywords are free. They cost no [[effect-tag|Effect Ranks]] and do not count toward the 10-tag cap. The price is not mechanical. The price is that your Power now has an identity — and identities can be read, countered, and exploited.

---

## What Keywords Do

Applying a Keyword to a Power activates two things:

**The [[specificity-modifier|Specificity Modifier]]:** Typed offensive effects deal at 2:1 (double rank value). Typed defensive effects deal at 1.5:1. This is a significant power increase for zero budget cost.

**Type matchup vulnerability:** The Power's Keyword is now subject to the Hexagon and Triangle Circuit systems. An advantaged matchup multiplies damage by 1.5×. A disadvantaged matchup cuts it to 0.5×. A typed Power can be devastating or underwhelming depending on what it's hitting.

---

## Keywords and Elements

Keywords map to the MRE's [[element|Element]] system — the elemental types associated with each character's [[vector|Vector]] (Enneagram type). The Hexagon Circuit and Triangle Circuit define advantage/disadvantage relationships between Elements. A character whose Keyword matches their Vector's native Element gains [[stab|STAB]] (Same-Type Attack Bonus) — additional effectiveness that rewards thematic consistency.

---

## The Generic Alternative

A Power without a Keyword operates at 1:1 — base output, no multiplier, no type matchup. Generic Powers are weaker in ceiling but completely uncounterable. No one can resist what has no type. The choice between typed and generic is the choice between explosive potential (with vulnerability) and reliable modesty (with safety).

This is [[creative-reciprocity|Creative Reciprocity]] at the Power level: the commitment to a specific identity (typing your Power) produces both greater strength and greater vulnerability simultaneously.

## You Died Variant

> **You Died sub-game:** In You Died, Keywords function identically on chip-based Power effects. The Specificity Modifier ratios and type matchup multipliers apply without modification.
