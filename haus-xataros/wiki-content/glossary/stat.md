# Stat

A Stat is the root of a character's identity in a domain — the single number that tells you how much Physical, Mental, or Social capacity they possess. Rated 1 to 5, each Stat determines the size of its corresponding [[pool|Pool]], the base of its [[dice-hand|Dice Hand]], and the character's ceiling in that domain. A Stat of 1 is functional. A Stat of 5 is extraordinary. And a Stat of 1 in a domain you neglected is a structural vulnerability your opponent *will* find.

---

## The Three Stats

| Stat | Domain | Governs |
|:-----|:-------|:--------|
| **Physical** | Body, strength, endurance, health | Melee attacks, physical resilience, feats of athletics |
| **Mental** | Intellect, willpower, focus, perception | Hacking, psychic actions, analytical challenges |
| **Social** | Charisma, composure, influence, presence | Rhetoric, intimidation, seduction, legal argument |

All three Stats are mechanically equivalent. The system does not privilege Physical over Social or Mental over Physical. A Social attack is as real as a sword strike, and Social [[damage|Damage]] triggers the same [[gate-state|Gate State]] as Physical damage.

---

## What Stats Determine

**[[pool|Pool]] size:** Pool = Stat × 6 + 6.

| Stat Rank | Pool |
|:---------:|:----:|
| 1 | 12 |
| 2 | 18 |
| 3 | 24 |
| 4 | 30 |
| 5 | 36 |

**Base [[dice-hand|Dice Hand]]:** The Stat provides the base dice before [[skill|Skill]] and equipment bonuses are added. A Physical Stat of 3 means 3 base dice for Physical actions.

**[[entropic-cap|Entropic Cap]] ceiling:** The Dice Hand cannot exceed the current Pool. Since the Pool is derived from the Stat, the Stat sets the upper boundary of everything — the maximum possible dice, the maximum possible Successes, the maximum possible [[threshold|Threshold]] from Defense allocation.

**Automatic [[skill|Skills]]:** At character creation, each Stat rank grants access to a number of domain-appropriate Skills. Higher Stats mean broader competence.

---

## Stat Costs

Stats are purchased at character creation with [[cp|CP (Character Points)]]. Costs escalate:

| Rank | Cumulative CP Cost |
|:----:|:------------------:|
| 1 | 1 |
| 2 | 2 |
| 3 | 5 |
| 4 | 9 |
| 5 | 14 |

Every character begins with a minimum of Rank 1 in all three Stats. The escalating cost means reaching Stat 5 in one domain requires significant investment — investment not available for the other two domains. Specialization is a choice with structural consequences.

---

## Dump Stats Are Real

A character with Physical 5 and Social 1 has a Physical Pool of 36 and a Social Pool of 12. Twelve Social hit points. A moderately invested Social attacker can push that character into Social [[gate-state|Gate State]] in two or three Beats, regardless of how physically dominant they are. The bruiser whose identity collapses under a well-crafted insult is a feature, not a bug — the MRE's three-domain system means no single Stat covers all vulnerabilities.

---

## You Died Variant

> **You Died sub-game:** In You Died, Stats determine starting chip pool sizes using the same formula (Stat × 6 + 6). The three-domain structure is identical. Chip allocation replaces dice allocation, but the Stat remains the root that determines everything downstream.
