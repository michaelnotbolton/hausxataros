# Scene Action

A Scene Action is anything a character does in a [[beat|Beat]] that isn't a [[power|Power]] — the non-supernatural, non-built actions that arise from the situation rather than the character sheet. Opening a door. Searching a room. Applying basic first aid without the Healing tag. Shoving a bookcase to block a corridor. Delivering a one-line taunt that isn't backed by a Social Power. Scene Actions are the improvisational layer of combat: the things characters do because the fiction demands it, not because they built for it.

---

## How Scene Actions Work

Scene Actions use the same resolution math as Power-based actions:

1. The character rolls appropriate Action dice from their [[dice-hand|Dice Hand]].
2. The Judge sets a [[threshold|Threshold]] based on difficulty (or the target rolls Defense dice, if opposed).
3. [[success|Successes]] are compared to Threshold. [[margin|Margin]] is calculated.
4. The [[failure-gradient|Failure Gradient]] applies on misses — a Catastrophic Failure on a Scene Action feeds the [[entropy-pool|Entropy Pool]] just as surely as a Catastrophic Failure in Power-based combat.

Relevant [[skill|Skills]] add dice to the roll. A character with Athletics 2 performing a combat sprint adds 4 Skill dice to their Physical Dice Hand for that action.

---

## Scene Actions vs. Powers

The key distinction: Scene Actions do not cost [[strain|Strain]], do not require [[affirmation|Affirmations]], and do not carry [[effect-tag|Effect Tags]]. They are simpler, cheaper, and weaker. A Scene Action punch always deals a minimum of 1 damage regardless of Margin (the basic attack rule), but it will never match the output of a purpose-built Attack Power with Damage tags and [[keyword|Keyword]] typing.

Scene Actions exist to ensure that characters always have *something* they can do — even when their Powers are on cooldown, when Strain has drained their Pool dangerously low, or when the situation calls for something no one thought to build for.

---

## You Died Variant

> **You Died sub-game:** In You Died, Scene Actions function identically — chip-draw resolution against Judge-set Thresholds, with no Strain cost and no Effect Tag bonuses. The same minimum-1-damage rule applies to basic physical actions.
