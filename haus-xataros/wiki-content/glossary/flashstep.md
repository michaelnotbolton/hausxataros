# Flashstep

Where [[tempo|Tempo]] sharpens a single act, Flashstep multiplies them. In the physics of belief, Flashstep is the decision to exist in more than one moment at once — each tier of it another branch of the same willful self, acting before, during, and alongside. It is expensive. Conviction spent on being in multiple places is conviction not spent on being right.

---

## What Flashstep Is

Flashstep is a purchase made from the [[momentum|Momentum]] sub-pool during the [[allocation-phase|Allocation Phase]]. It grants a character additional actions within a single Beat, each resolving at a progressively faster [[time-bands|Time Band]].

Unlike [[tempo|Tempo]], which moves a single action, Flashstep adds new actions at faster bands while preserving the character's default General-band action.

---

## The Cascade Structure

Each Flashstep tier purchased grants one action at that tier's priority level — and the character continues to act at *every tier below*, down through General.

| Flashstep Tier | Total Actions | Priority Levels |
|---------------|--------------|----------------|
| None | 1 | General |
| **Tier 1** | **2** | Priority +1, then General |
| **Tier 2** | **3** | Priority +2, then Priority +1, then General |
| **Tier 3** | **4** | Priority +3, then Priority +2, then Priority +1, then General |

This is the cascade's defining property. Buying Flashstep Tier 2 does not give you one fast action — it gives you three actions, each faster than the last. You act at the top, then again at the next band down, then again at General. The character genuinely exists at multiple moments in the Beat.

Higher tiers can be purchased, though each additional tier is exponentially more expensive and leaves progressively less Momentum available for [[tempo|Tempo]], Defense, or Action amplification.

## Cost Structure

Flashstep purchases escalate in cost per tier. The specific dice costs are **TBD pending playtest tuning.** The cascade structure (each tier adds one action at that priority, preserving all lower actions) is locked; the price curve is the open variable.

Baseline assumption from prior playtest work: approximately 2 Momentum dice for Flashstep Tier 1, scaling upward. Exact numbers pending the next tournament cycle.

## Flashstep vs. Tempo — The Speed/Volume Tradeoff

Both mechanics pull from the same [[momentum|Momentum]] budget. They differ in what they deliver:

**Tempo** moves one action to a faster band. You act once, very fast.

**Flashstep** multiplies actions across bands. You act multiple times at progressively faster priorities.

Tempo is more cost-efficient for reaching a high priority tier. Flashstep is less efficient per tier but scales output — a Flashstep Tier 2 character lands three swings in a single Beat, which can demolish a target's multi-pool defense faster than any single hit.

Combining both is possible but punishing on the Momentum budget. A character who buys Flashstep Tier 2 *and* spends Tempo for additional priority advantage has committed nearly the entire Dice Hand to speed, leaving a thin Action Roll and minimal Defense Threshold. Viable against a committed glass cannon; suicidal against a patient defender.

---

## Interaction with the Entropic Cap

The [[entropic-cap|Entropic Cap]] tightens Flashstep's cost as the fight progresses. Because the total Dice Hand cannot exceed current Pool, an injured character's Flashstep options shrink with them. A character at half Pool may not have enough dice available to buy the same Flashstep tier they afforded at full health, let alone purchase Tempo and Defense alongside it.

This is the desperation ramp the MRE models intentionally: action economy is a luxury of the living.

---

## Strategic Notes

**Flashstep thrives against multi-pool targets.** If you can attack different pools with different actions in the same Beat — Physical on the first strike, Mental on the second, Social on the third — you force the opponent's single Defense allocation to choose which pressure to answer.

**Tempo-boosted single attacks often beat high-tier Flashstep against single-pool opponents.** One lethal hit lands first; three medium hits trade damage.

**The opportunity cost is Defense.** A character who buys Flashstep Tier 2 has allocated so much Momentum that their Defense dice are likely zero or near-zero. Their Threshold is whatever their targeted Stat Rank provides, no more. This is the inverse of the Flashstep ceiling: your action economy is enormous, your durability is minimal.

## You Died Variant

> **You Died sub-game:** In You Died, Flashstep purchases are made with chips. Each chip tier grants one additional card-play at that priority, following the same cascade structure. Card draw effects can occasionally grant free Flashstep tiers (the Ripple card and specific Geist trigger cards provide this). See [[you-died/flashstep-yd]] for chip costs and card interactions.
