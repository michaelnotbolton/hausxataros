# Entropy Pool

The Entropy Pool is the universe pushing back. It is a Judge-side resource — a pool of dice that grows when players fail spectacularly and can be spent to introduce complications, empower NPCs, or trigger environmental hazards. The Entropy Pool represents the MRE's fundamental stance on disorder: it is not random, it is accumulated. Every Catastrophic Failure feeds it. Every feed makes the world more dangerous. The characters are not fighting in a vacuum. They are fighting in a system that remembers their worst moments and weaponizes them.

---

## How It Grows

The Entropy Pool gains dice from **Catastrophic Failures** — rolls where the negative [[margin|Margin]] reaches −5 or worse (see [[failure-gradient|Failure Gradient]]). When a character fails that badly, the Judge describes the disaster and adds dice to the Entropy Pool.

The [[entropic-cap|Entropic Cap]] creates a feedback loop: as characters take [[damage|Damage]], their [[pool|Pools]] shrink, their [[dice-hand|Dice Hands]] shrink, and their rolls become weaker — making Catastrophic Failures more likely, which feeds the Entropy Pool, which makes the situation worse. The death spiral has a communal dimension.

---

## How It's Spent

The Judge spends Entropy Pool dice at their discretion to:

- **Introduce complications** — the terrain shifts, a door slams shut, reinforcements arrive.
- **Empower NPCs** — a boss gains a temporary buff, a minion gets a second wind.
- **Trigger environmental hazards** — the floor collapses, the fire spreads, the power grid surges.

The Entropy Pool is not targeted at the character who caused it. It is a *communal* threat — one character's spectacular failure can result in a complication that hits a different character entirely. Team coordination matters even in failure.

---

## You Died Variant

> **You Died sub-game:** In You Died, the Entropy Pool is replaced by a card-based Entropy reserve the Dealer draws from to introduce complications. Catastrophic Failures feed it identically.
