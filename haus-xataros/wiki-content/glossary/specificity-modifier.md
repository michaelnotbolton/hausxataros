# Specificity Modifier

The Specificity Modifier is [[creative-reciprocity|Creative Reciprocity]] given teeth. When a [[power|Power]] carries a [[keyword|Keyword]] — an elemental type that narrows it from "generic force" to "this specific kind of force" — the system doubles down: typed offensive effects deal **2:1** (double their rank value), typed defensive effects operate at **1.5:1** (150% value), and generic (unkeyworded) effects stay at **1:1** (base value, uncounterable). The modifier is free. It costs no [[effect-tag|Effect Ranks]]. The price is strategic: your Power now has an elemental identity that can be exploited through the type matchup circuits.

---

## The Multipliers

| Power Type | Keyword Status | Output Ratio |
|:-----------|:---------------|:------------:|
| Offensive (e.g., Damage tag) | Typed | **2:1** — Damage Rank 2 = +8 instead of +4 |
| Defensive (e.g., DR, Shield) | Typed | **1.5:1** — DR Rank 2 = −6 instead of −4 |
| Any | Generic (no Keyword) | **1:1** — base value, no multiplier |

Then apply the type matchup from the [[hexagon-circuit|Hexagon]] and [[triangle-circuit|Triangle Circuit]]:

| Matchup | Damage Multiplier |
|:--------|:-----------------:|
| Advantaged element | × 1.5 |
| Neutral | × 1.0 |
| Disadvantaged element | × 0.5 |

The full swing between best case (typed, 2:1, advantaged) and worst case (typed, 2:1, disadvantaged) is **3:1**. A Damage Rank 2 attack deals +8 against a neutral target, +12 against an advantaged matchup, or +4 against a disadvantaged one. This is why typing matters. This is why information matters.

---

## The Generic Tradeoff

Generic Powers operate at 1:1 — strictly lower output than typed effects. But they carry no elemental identity, which means they cannot be countered through type matchups. A generic attack always deals its base value, regardless of the target's elemental resistances. The tradeoff is reliability vs. ceiling: generic effects are consistent but modest; typed effects are devastating or underwhelming depending on the matchup.

---

## The Gate Rule Relaxation

The Specificity Modifier also affects the [[gate-rule|Gate Rule]]. Normally, [[exotic-tag|Exotic Tags]] can only target characters in [[gate-state|Gate State]] (Pool = 0). But typed Exotic effects relax this threshold to **25% of the relevant Pool** — allowing them to target characters who are badly hurt but not yet at zero. This makes typed Exotic effects more accessible but also more counterable, extending the same Creative Reciprocity logic into the endgame.

---

## You Died Variant

> **You Died sub-game:** In You Died, the Specificity Modifier applies identically to chip-based Power effects. The 2:1, 1.5:1, and 1:1 ratios and the type matchup multipliers are unchanged.
