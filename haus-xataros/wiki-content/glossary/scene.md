# Scene

A Scene is the dramatic container — a continuous stretch of play unified by a location, a situation, and a question the fiction needs to answer. In combat, a Scene is composed of multiple [[beat|Beats]] and ends when the fight concludes (through elimination, surrender, or narrative resolution). Outside combat, a Scene is however long the Judge and players need it to be. Gate State is scene removal: the character is out for the remainder of *this* Scene, not permanently (unless an [[exotic-tag|Exotic Tag]] like Execution says otherwise).

---

## Scene as Boundary

Several mechanics use the Scene as their scope:

- **[[gate-state|Gate State]]** removes a character for the Scene.
- **Resurrection** (an [[exotic-tag|Exotic Tag]]) must target a character who died within the current Scene.
- **Cooldowns** on certain [[power|Powers]] may reset between Scenes.
- **Temporary effects** that last "until end of Scene" expire when the Scene closes.

The Scene is the MRE's medium-scale unit of dramatic time — larger than a Beat, smaller than a [[session|Session]].

---

## You Died Variant

> **You Died sub-game:** In You Died, Scenes function identically as the boundary for Gate State removal, cooldown resets, and effect durations.
