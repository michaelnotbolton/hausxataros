# Effect Rank

An Effect Rank is a unit of budget within a single [[power|Power]] — the currency spent to purchase [[effect-tag|Effect Tags]] during Power construction. Each tag costs one or more Effect Ranks: a Damage tag at Rank 2 costs 2 Effect Ranks. A Domination tag costs 5. The total number of Effect Ranks available to a Power is determined by a simple formula:

> **Effect Ranks = [[intensity|Intensity]] + [[restriction-bonus|Restriction Bonus]]**

A 3-Intensity Power with 2 Restrictions has approximately 5 Effect Ranks. A 1-Intensity Power with 4 Restrictions also has 5. The budget is the same. The cost to use them — the [[strain|Strain]] — is not.

---

## Budget Constraints

Effect Ranks are spent exclusively on Effect Tags. A Power may contain a maximum of **10 Effect Tags** (Restrictions do not count toward this cap). Each tag can be taken at multiple ranks for scaling effect — Damage Rank 1 adds +2 damage, Rank 2 adds +4, Rank 3 adds +6. Higher ranks of the same tag cost additional Effect Ranks.

The budget is always finite. Every rank spent on offense is a rank not available for defense, control, or utility. Power construction is resource allocation under constraint — the same design philosophy that governs the [[allocation-phase|Allocation Phase]].

---

## You Died Variant

> **You Died sub-game:** In You Died, Effect Ranks function identically within chip-based Power construction. The Intensity + Restriction Bonus formula and tag cost structure are unchanged.
