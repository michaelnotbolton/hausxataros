# Strain

Every Power used in combat costs something — and Strain is the bill. When a character activates a [[power|Power]], Strain dice are rolled during the [[cleanup-phase|Cleanup Phase]] and the total is subtracted from their designated Strain [[pool|Pool]]. This is self-damage. It comes from inside. It cannot be healed in the same Beat it was dealt. Strain is the MRE's thermodynamic law: power is never free. The question is not whether you pay, but how much, and how long you can afford to keep paying.

---

## The Formula

> **Strain Dice = [[intensity|Intensity]] − [[restriction|Restriction]] discounts (minimum 1d6)**

- A Power with Intensity 3 and 2 Restrictions: 3 − 2 = **1d6 Strain**.
- A Power with Intensity 5 and 0 Restrictions: 5 − 0 = **5d6 Strain**.
- A Power with Intensity 1 and 4 Restrictions: 1 − 4 = still **1d6 Strain** (minimum floor).

The minimum is always 1d6. No matter how many Restrictions a character stacks, using a Power always costs something. The floor is non-negotiable.

---

## When Strain Hits

Strain is rolled during Step 1 of the [[cleanup-phase|Cleanup Phase]], after all actions for the Beat have resolved. The dice are rolled, the total is summed, and the result is subtracted from the Power's designated Strain Pool.

**The Strain Pool designation** is chosen during Power construction (Step 7 of the 8-step algorithm). A Physical attack does not have to route Strain to the Physical Pool — a character could designate their Social Pool as the Strain sink, burning composure to fuel violence. The designation is permanent once built.

---

## The Thermodynamic Law

> **Healing effects cannot restore Strain damage dealt in the same Beat.**

If a character uses a 3d6 Strain Power and a teammate heals them in the same Beat, the heal applies to damage taken from enemies — not from their own Strain. The cost of power cannot be circumvented within the same time window. The engine enforces this absolutely.

---

## Strain and the Restriction Economy

Strain is what makes [[restriction|Restrictions]] meaningful, not punitive. A character who builds Powers with many Restrictions pays less Strain per use — their Powers are cheap to activate but conditional, exploitable, full of requirements an informed opponent can deny. A character who builds Powers with no Restrictions pays massive Strain — their Powers work everywhere, against everything, with no vulnerability, but the self-damage limits how many Beats they can sustain before their own Pool collapses.

A character with a Pool of 24 using a 1d6 Strain Power can sustain approximately 6–7 Beats of continuous use before entering [[gate-state|Gate State]] from self-damage alone (assuming average rolls and no external damage). A character using a 5d6 Strain Power lasts approximately 1–2 Beats. The math is brutal and intentional.

---

## Strain as Spontaneous Evolution Fuel

Strain damage counts toward [[spontaneous-evolution|Spontaneous Evolution]]. A character who takes 4 points of Strain in a Beat rolls those 4d6 alongside any enemy-inflicted damage during the Spontaneous Evolution step. Each 6 produces 1 XP. The character pays in blood — and might learn something from the bleeding.

---

## You Died Variant

> **You Died sub-game:** In You Died, Strain subtracts chips from the designated chip pool rather than subtracting numeric damage from a Pool. The Intensity-minus-Restrictions formula and minimum floor apply identically. The Thermodynamic Law prevents same-Beat chip recovery from Strain.
