# Effect Tag

An Effect Tag is a mechanical component that defines what a [[power|Power]] actually *does* — the specific capability purchased with [[effect-rank|Effect Ranks]] during Power construction. A Power without Effect Tags is a concept with no execution. The tags are the execution. Each one costs one or more Effect Ranks, and together they determine whether the Power deals [[damage|Damage]], creates barriers, heals wounds, controls movement, or does something far stranger. A single Power can hold up to 10 Effect Tags. [[restriction|Restrictions]] do not count toward this cap.

---

## The Seven Categories

Effect Tags are organized into seven functional categories:

| Category | Examples | Role |
|:---------|:---------|:-----|
| **Offensive** | Damage, Piercing, Multi-Target | Deal damage or expand offensive reach |
| **Defensive** | Armor, Shield, [[damage-reduction|DR]] | Reduce or prevent incoming damage |
| **Control** | Immobilize, Stun, Barrier | Restrict enemy options and movement |
| **Enhancement/Debilitation** | Boost, Weaken | Modify Stats or capabilities (self or target) |
| **Utility** | Movement, Perception, Healing | Provide non-combat or support functions |
| **[[exotic-tag|Exotic]]** | Domination, Transformation, Erasure, Execution | Devastating effects locked behind [[gate-state|Gate State]] |
| **Meta** | [[specificity-modifier|Specificity Modifier]], Volatile, Range/Duration Extension | Modify the Power's properties rather than adding direct effects |

---

## Cost and Budget

Each Effect Tag costs one or more Effect Ranks. A Power's total Effect Rank budget equals [[intensity|Intensity]] + [[restriction-bonus|Restriction Bonus]]. Building a Power is the art of fitting the right tags into the available budget — every rank spent on Damage is a rank not available for Control or Utility.

---

## You Died Variant

> **You Died sub-game:** In You Died, Effect Tags function identically within chip-based Power construction. Tag categories, rank costs, and the 10-tag cap are unchanged.
