# Creative Reciprocity

Creative Reciprocity is Foundational Law 1 of the MRE — the philosophical statement the entire engine is built on: **specificity produces both power and vulnerability simultaneously.** Any effect narrowed through a [[keyword|Keyword]] doubles its output via the [[specificity-modifier|Specificity Modifier]], but becomes targetable and counterable through the type matchup circuits. Any [[power|Power]] loaded with [[restriction|Restrictions]] gains [[restriction-bonus|Restriction Bonus]] Effect Ranks and reduced [[strain|Strain]], but every Restriction is a condition an informed enemy can exploit. Generic, unrestricted effects are modest and reliable and uncounterable. The engine does not punish commitment to identity. It rewards it — and then makes it dangerous.

---

## The Principle in Action

Creative Reciprocity manifests everywhere in the MRE:

**In typing:** A Keyword doubles offensive output (2:1) but opens the Power to type matchup disadvantages (0.5× against resistant targets). The swing between best case and worst case is 3:1. Information about an opponent's typing becomes a strategic weapon.

**In Restrictions:** Each Restriction adds +1 Effect Rank and reduces Strain cost — but each is also a condition the enemy can deny. The [[creative-reciprocity|1 CP Nuke]] demonstrates the extreme: a 1-Intensity Power with 4 Restrictions matches a 5-Intensity Power in output, at one-fifth the Strain cost, but with four exploitable conditions.

**In [[charge|Charge]]:** Charge delays a Power's activation to a later [[time-bands|Time Band]] in exchange for Restriction Bonus. The Power hits harder but arrives later — and every faster action in the Beat has a chance to disrupt it.

**In [[exotic-tag|Exotic Tags]]:** The most devastating effects in the system require the target to already be in [[gate-state|Gate State]]. Maximum power, maximum prerequisite.

---

## The 1 CP Nuke

Foundational Law 2 extends Creative Reciprocity into a mathematical proof: a 1-CP Power with 4 Restrictions produces the same 5 Effect Ranks as a 5-CP Power with 0 Restrictions — at 1d6 Strain instead of 5d6. Restrictions are not penalties. They are the character's mythology made mechanical. The system ensures that creativity and commitment to identity are rewarded as much as raw investment.

## You Died Variant

> **You Died sub-game:** In You Died, Creative Reciprocity operates identically — chip-based Power construction follows the same Restriction/Strain/typing tradeoffs. The foundational law is engine-level, not format-specific.
