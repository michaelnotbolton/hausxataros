# XP (Experience Points)

XP is the advancement currency — the resource earned during play that lets characters grow, adapt, and rebuild themselves in response to what they've survived. The primary source is [[spontaneous-evolution|Spontaneous Evolution]]: every point of [[damage|Damage]] taken generates a d6 roll, and every 6 produces 1 XP, available immediately. XP can also come from story milestones at the Judge's discretion. Unlike [[cp|CP]], which is spent once at creation, XP flows continuously — most dramatically in combat, where the character taking the most punishment is the character with the most potential for reinvention.

---

## What XP Buys

| Purchase | Cost |
|:---------|:-----|
| New [[power|Power]] | 2 XP per [[intensity|Intensity]] point |
| [[stat|Stat]] increase | Varies (follows CP cost table) |
| [[skill|Skill]] rank | 2 XP per rank |
| Type Advancement | Varies |
| Wing Unlock | 10 XP |
| Growth Integration | 30 XP |
| Disadvantage buyoff | 2 XP |

The most dramatic use is **mid-combat Power construction** — spending Spontaneous Evolution XP during the [[planning-phase|Planning Phase]] to build a new Power tailored to the fight in progress.

---

## You Died Variant

> **You Died sub-game:** In You Died, XP is earned and spent identically. Chip-loss triggers Spontaneous Evolution rolls, and earned XP purchases Powers and advancement at the same rates.
