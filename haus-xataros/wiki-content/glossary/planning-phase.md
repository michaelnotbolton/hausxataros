# Planning Phase

The Planning Phase is the breath before the commitment — Phase 1 of the [[poker-hand-model|Poker-Hand Model]], where players speak openly, toggle their tools, and spend whatever growth the last Beat's pain produced. Everything said here is public. The Judge hears it. Clever NPCs in earshot may react to it. But the MRE rewards coordinated play, so the trade-off is intentional: the team that plans together fights together, even if their enemies are listening.

---

## What Happens

Four activities occur during the Planning Phase, in any order:

**Strategy discussion.** Players talk to each other openly about what they intend to do. This is collaborative and encouraged — but it is not private. The information shared at the table is shared with the table.

**Persistent State toggles.** Any [[power|Powers]] with the Persistent State tag (always-on abilities like energy shields, enhanced senses, or aura effects) can be switched on or off. Toggling is free but must be declared before the [[allocation-phase|Allocation Phase]] begins.

**[[spontaneous-evolution|Spontaneous Evolution]] spending.** If any character earned [[xp|XP]] during the previous Beat's [[cleanup-phase|Cleanup Phase]] (from taking [[damage|Damage]]), they may spend that XP now — on new Powers (2 XP per [[intensity|Intensity]]), [[stat|Stat]] increases, [[skill|Skill]] ranks, or other XP purchases. The most dramatic use: building a brand-new Power mid-combat, tailored to counter the threat that just hurt you.

**[[flashstep|Flashstep]] intentions.** Players may declare that they intend to take multiple actions this Beat. They do not need to specify how many yet — just the intention, so other players can plan around it.

---

## The Transition

Once all planning is complete, the game moves to the [[allocation-phase|Allocation Phase]]. From this point forward, all decisions are secret and irrevocable. The Planning Phase is the last moment of open information before the blind bet.

---

## You Died Variant

> **You Died sub-game:** In You Died, the Planning Phase functions identically. Strategy discussion is open, Persistent State card effects can be toggled, and XP from Spontaneous Evolution can be spent on chip-based Power construction. The transition to hidden allocation follows the same structure.
