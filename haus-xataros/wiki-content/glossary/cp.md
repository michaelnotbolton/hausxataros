# CP (Character Points)

CP is the creation currency — the budget a player spends at character construction to define who their character *is* before the first scene begins. CP purchases [[stat|Stats]], [[power|Powers]] (via [[intensity|Intensity]]), and [[skill|Skills]]. It is separated from [[ip|IP (Item Points)]] at creation to prevent dump-stacking all resources into combat powers while ignoring equipment. Once the character enters play, CP is spent. Further advancement comes through [[xp|XP]], earned primarily via [[spontaneous-evolution|Spontaneous Evolution]].

---

## What CP Buys

| Purchase | Cost |
|:---------|:-----|
| Stat Rank 1 | 1 CP |
| Stat Rank 2 | 2 CP (cumulative) |
| Stat Rank 3 | 5 CP (cumulative) |
| Stat Rank 4 | 9 CP (cumulative) |
| Stat Rank 5 | 14 CP (cumulative) |
| Power Intensity | 1 CP per Intensity point |
| Skill Ranks | Varies (see [[skill|Skill]]) |

The escalating Stat costs mean that maximizing one Stat (Rank 5 = 14 CP) consumes a large share of the creation budget, leaving less for Powers and the other two domains. Every CP spent is a CP unavailable elsewhere. The budget *is* the identity.

---

## You Died Variant

> **You Died sub-game:** In You Died, character construction uses the same CP budget and cost tables. Chip pool sizes are derived from Stats purchased with CP using the standard formula (Stat × 6 + 6).
