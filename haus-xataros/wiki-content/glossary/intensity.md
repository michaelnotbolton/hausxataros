# Intensity

Intensity is the raw investment in a [[power|Power]] — the amount of [[cp|CP]] poured into it at creation, or [[xp|XP]] spent on it during play (at 2 XP per Intensity point). It is the base that everything else builds on. Intensity determines the starting budget of [[effect-tag|Effect Ranks]], the base [[strain|Strain]] cost, and the ceiling of what the Power can do before [[restriction-bonus|Restriction Bonuses]] expand it. A 1-Intensity Power is a precision tool. A 5-Intensity Power is a statement of commitment. Both are valid. Both cost something.

---

## What Intensity Determines

**Effect Rank budget:** A Power's base Effect Ranks equal its Intensity. A 3-Intensity Power starts with 3 Effect Ranks before Restriction Bonuses are added.

**Strain cost:** A Power's base Strain dice equal its Intensity, minus [[restriction|Restriction]] discounts (minimum 1d6). Higher Intensity = more self-damage per use, unless offset by Restrictions.

**Total budget with Restrictions:** Effect Ranks = Intensity + Restriction Bonus. A 1-Intensity Power with 4 Restrictions has 5 total Effect Ranks (1 + 4). A 5-Intensity Power with 0 Restrictions also has 5 Effect Ranks — but costs 5d6 Strain instead of 1d6.

---

## Cost Scaling

| Source | Cost per Intensity |
|:-------|:------------------:|
| Character creation | 1 [[cp|CP]] per Intensity |
| During play / [[spontaneous-evolution|Spontaneous Evolution]] | 2 [[xp|XP]] per Intensity |

The XP cost during play means mid-combat Power construction favors low-Intensity builds supplemented by Restrictions — which is exactly the [[creative-reciprocity|1 CP Nuke]] principle in action.

---

## You Died Variant

> **You Died sub-game:** In You Died, Intensity determines chip-cost budget for Power construction using the same formulas. XP-based mid-combat construction follows identical scaling.
