# Success

A Success is a die that landed true. On any d6 roll in the MRE, a result of 5 or 6 counts as one Success. Everything else — the 1s, the 2s, the 3s, the 4s — is noise. The system does not care how high your Success rolled or how low your failure fell. It cares only whether you crossed the line. Count your Successes. Compare them to the [[threshold|Threshold]]. The difference — the [[margin|Margin]] — is the magnitude of what you accomplished or how badly you fell short.

---

## The Math

The probability of Success on a single d6 is **2 in 6 (33.3%)**. This means:

- A pool of 6 dice produces an average of **2 Successes**.
- A pool of 12 dice produces an average of **4 Successes**.
- A pool of 24 dice produces an average of **8 Successes**.

The MRE uses only d6 dice. There are no d8s, d10s, or d20s. Every roll in the system — attacks, defenses, skill checks, [[strain|Strain]] — uses the same die, the same Success threshold, the same binary: 5 or 6, or not.

---

## Where Successes Matter

Successes are counted in every phase of combat that involves dice:

- **Action rolls** — Successes compared to [[threshold|Threshold]] to determine hit or miss, then [[margin|Margin]] for damage.
- **Defense rolls** — Successes from Defense dice set the Threshold the attacker must overcome (Threshold = 1 + Defense Successes).
- **[[momentum|Momentum]] rolls** — Successes determine action order within each [[time-bands|Time Band]].
- **Skill checks** — Successes compared to Judge-set Threshold for non-combat actions.

---

## You Died Variant

> **You Died sub-game:** In You Died, the Success mechanic is replaced by chip-draw evaluation. The binary pass/fail structure remains, but the probability curve shifts based on chip composition rather than die faces.
