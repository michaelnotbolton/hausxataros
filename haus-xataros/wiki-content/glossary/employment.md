# Employment

Employment is the cost of using your gear. When a character uses an equipped [[item|Item]] during a [[beat|Beat]], they subtract 1 die from their [[dice-hand|Dice Hand]] per Item employed — paid *before* the [[allocation-phase|Allocation Phase]] begins, reducing the total hand available for division into [[momentum|Momentum]], Defense, and Action. A character employing a sword (−1) and a shield (−1) loses 2 dice from their hand before they even begin deciding how to spend the rest. Equipment grants power, but wielding it demands attention.

---

## The Net Calculation

Items have a Quality rating (Q1–Q4) that adds dice to the Dice Hand. Employment subtracts 1. The net gain from an Item is Quality minus 1:

| Item Quality | Dice Added | Employment Cost | Net Gain |
|:------------:|:----------:|:---------------:|:--------:|
| Q1 | +1 | −1 | 0 |
| Q2 | +2 | −1 | +1 |
| Q3 | +3 | −1 | +2 |
| Q4 | +4 | −1 | +3 |

A Q1 Item breaks even — it exists for its tags and narrative function, not its dice. Higher Quality Items produce genuine mechanical advantage, but each employed Item still costs 1 die of attention.

---

## Employment Limits

A character may equip a maximum of **3 Items**. Employing all three costs 3 dice before allocation. On a small Dice Hand (from a low [[stat|Stat]] or a [[pool|Pool]] degraded by the [[entropic-cap|Entropic Cap]]), that 3-die tax can be devastating — leaving almost nothing for the three-bucket division.

---

## You Died Variant

> **You Died sub-game:** In You Died, Employment costs 1 chip per Item employed, subtracted from the domain-specific chip pool before allocation. The net-gain calculation is identical.
