# Item

An Item is a piece of equipment with mechanical properties — a sword, a shield, a hacking rig, a courtroom badge — built using the item construction system and purchased with [[ip|IP (Item Points)]] at character creation. Items have a Quality rating (Q1–Q4) that adds dice to the [[dice-hand|Dice Hand]], a Durability Pool that tracks wear, and may carry tags and complications of their own. Using an Item in combat requires [[employment|Employment]] — subtracting 1 die from the Dice Hand per Item employed. A character may equip a maximum of **3 Items**.

---

## Quality

| Quality | Dice Added | Net Gain (after Employment) |
|:-------:|:----------:|:---------------------------:|
| Q1 | +1 | 0 |
| Q2 | +2 | +1 |
| Q3 | +3 | +2 |
| Q4 | +4 | +3 |

A Q1 Item breaks even on dice — its value lies in its tags and narrative function. Higher Quality Items provide genuine mechanical advantage, but the [[employment|Employment]] cost means every equipped Item still demands attention from the Dice Hand.

---

## Acquisition

Items are purchased at character creation with [[ip|IP]], which is separated from [[cp|CP]] to prevent dump-stacking all creation resources into combat [[power|Powers]]. After creation, Items can be acquired through gameplay, crafting, or [[xp|XP]] expenditure. The starting IP budget is **15 IP**.

---

## You Died Variant

> **You Died sub-game:** In You Died, Items add bonus chips to the domain-specific chip pool, with the same Employment cost (1 chip per Item employed). Quality ratings and the 3-Item equip cap are unchanged.
