# Reveal Phase

The Reveal Phase is the moment of truth — Phase 3 of the [[poker-hand-model|Poker-Hand Model]], when every hidden allocation becomes public simultaneously. Every player sees how every other player divided their [[dice-hand|Dice Hand]]. The character who loaded [[momentum|Momentum]] for speed. The character who stacked Defense to survive. The character who poured everything into Action for one devastating strike. All of it, visible at once, unchangeable. The Reveal is where you find out whether your read of the opponent was right — and whether theirs was right about you.

---

## What Happens

All [[allocation-phase|Allocation Phase]] decisions are revealed simultaneously. Players uncover their written allocations, lift their screens, or uncup their dice. The Judge reveals NPC allocations at the same time.

**No changes can be made after the Reveal.** The allocation is locked. If you realize you misread the situation — if the opponent stacked the exact counter to your strategy — you live with it. The commitment was the decision. The Reveal is just the discovery of its consequences.

---

## What the Reveal Tells You

From the revealed allocations, every combatant now knows:

- **Who is fast** — high Momentum allocations signal [[tempo|Tempo]] or [[flashstep|Flashstep]] purchases, meaning those characters will act at higher [[time-bands|Time Bands]].
- **Who is defended** — high Defense allocations signal a high [[threshold|Threshold]], making those characters harder to damage.
- **Who is swinging** — high Action allocations signal a powerful offensive, but likely at the cost of speed or survival.
- **Who is vulnerable** — any allocation with 0 or near-0 in a bucket reveals a structural gap the opponent chose to accept.

This information matters for future Beats. The Reveal builds the mental model each player carries into the next [[planning-phase|Planning Phase]].

---

## You Died Variant

> **You Died sub-game:** In You Died, the Reveal Phase functions identically — all chip allocations are shown simultaneously. The information gained and the no-change rule apply without modification.
