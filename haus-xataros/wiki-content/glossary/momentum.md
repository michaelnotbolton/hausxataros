# Momentum

In Jotunnberg, belief is physics — and Momentum is the forward edge of yours. These are the dice you commit before resolution to velocity rather than endurance or precision. Spend Momentum on [[tempo|Tempo]] and your intent arrives before your opponent's. Spend it on [[flashstep|Flashstep]] and it arrives in more than one place at once. Spend nothing, and you meet the field on equal terms — which, against certain opponents, is the wiser gamble.

---

## What Momentum Is

During the [[allocation-phase|Allocation Phase]], every character secretly divides their [[dice-hand|Dice Hand]] into three buckets:

- **Momentum** — dice reserved for speed and pressure
- **Defense** — dice that set your [[threshold|Threshold]] against incoming attacks
- **Action** — dice that power your attack or ability roll

Momentum is the only sub-pool that can purchase [[tempo|Tempo]] and [[flashstep|Flashstep]]. It cannot be spent on anything else, and unused Momentum dice do not carry forward — what you set aside must be spent within the Beat or lost.

This allocation is made face-down, simultaneously with all other combatants, and revealed all at once in the Reveal Phase. Your opponent does not know how much you committed to speed until the moment everyone shows their hand.

## The Two Things Momentum Buys

### [[tempo|Tempo]]

Tempo is priority manipulation — a way to move a single action to a faster point in the resolution order without taking an additional action.

**Conversion rate:** 3 Momentum dice = 1 Tempo. *(Provisional — subject to playtest tuning.)*

**What Tempo does, by amount purchased:**

**1 Tempo — Priority Advantage**
Your action resolves at Priority +1, ahead of all actions in the same [[time-bands|Time Band]] that did not purchase Tempo. However: if two opponents both spend 1 Tempo for actions in the same Time Band, they cancel each other out — both are at Priority +1, so they resolve simultaneously again. Priority escalation is relative, not absolute.

**2+ Tempo — Time Band Escalation**
Instead of winning priority within a band, you move your action up to the next faster Time Band entirely. Each additional Tempo purchased pushes the action one band higher on the [[time-bands|Time Band]] ladder:

| Time Band | Tempo to reach from General |
|-----------|----------------------------|
| End of Beat (EoB) | — (slowest; Charge 2+ actions) |
| ASAP | — (Charge 1 actions) |
| General | 0 (default for uncharged actions) |
| Priority +1 | 1 Tempo |
| Priority +2 | 2 Tempo |
| Priority +3 | 3 Tempo |

*(Higher Flashstep tiers occupy Priority +2, +3, etc. Tempo lets a single action reach those bands without purchasing Flashstep — cheaper, but only one action instead of the cascade Flashstep provides.)*

**Tempo as Charge Buyoff**
Tempo can also be spent to buy off the [[charge|Charge]] tag's Time Band restrictions. A power with Charge 1 (ASAP band) can be pushed to General resolution for 1 Tempo, or to Priority +1 for 2 Tempo. Each Tempo purchase moves the charged action one band faster. *(Rate provisional: 1 Tempo per band step.)*

---

### [[flashstep|Flashstep]]

Flashstep is action multiplication — a way to take more than one action in a single Beat, each at a progressively faster priority.

**How Flashstep tiers work:**

Each Flashstep tier purchased adds one action at that priority level. Crucially, buying a higher Flashstep tier gives you actions at *all* the tiers below it as well — you do not take just one action at the top tier, you take one action at every tier from the top down through General.

| Flashstep Tier | Actions gained | Priority levels |
|---------------|----------------|-----------------|
| None | 1 action | General only |
| Flashstep 1 | 2 actions | Priority +1, then General |
| Flashstep 2 | 3 actions | Priority +2, then +1, then General |
| Flashstep 3 | 4 actions | Priority +3, then +2, then +1, then General |

This cascade structure means that purchasing a high Flashstep tier is enormously powerful — but it devours Momentum dice at an escalating rate, leaving very little for Defense or Action amplification.

**Tempo vs. Flashstep — the key tradeoff:**
Tempo can push a single action to Priority +2 or higher (matching the speed of a high Flashstep) for fewer Momentum dice — but you only get the one action. A character who buys Flashstep tier 2 acts *three times* at three different priority levels; a character who spends equivalent Tempo is very fast but acts *once*. Speed of arrival vs. volume of pressure. Both have their moment.

## The Momentum Allocation Decision

Because Momentum, Defense, and Action compete for the same Dice Hand, committing heavily to speed always has a cost. A character who loads Momentum to buy Flashstep tier 2 and high Tempo has very few dice left for Defense (a low Threshold, easy to hit) and a thin Action Roll (fewer dice to amplify through Affirmation and STAB). Jotunnberg rewards characters who read their opponent's likely commitment and allocate accordingly — not those who always maximize any single metric.

The [[entropic-cap|Entropic Cap]] tightens this further: as a character takes damage and their Pool shrinks, their total Dice Hand shrinks with it. An injured character cannot afford the Momentum investment they could make at full health. Speed is a luxury of the living.

## You Died Variant

> **You Died sub-game:** In You Died, the dice allocation model is replaced by poker chip spending, supplemented by cards drawn during play. The Momentum bucket concept translates to a dedicated chip pool spent on speed and extra actions rather than on attack or defense. The Flashstep cascade structure and Tempo priority mechanics operate on the same logic, but with chip costs instead of die counts. Specific You Died costs are defined in the [[you-died/flashstep-yd|You Died Flashstep]] and [[you-died/tempo-yd|You Died Tempo]] articles.
